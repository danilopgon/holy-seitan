# Holy Seitan — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
María (@mloherr) and Dani (@danilopgon) — two developers who are vegan and cook together. The app is publicly accessible but built entirely for their own use; no external audience to impress. They use it regularly as a personal reference: browsing recipes, checking ingredients and steps while cooking. The emotional goal is comfort and ease — a tool that feels like home.

### Brand Personality
**Cozy, geeky, personal.**

Voice is casual Spanish, warm but precise. The tone is that of a well-worn notebook a developer would carry — functional and opinionated, with personality in the details (emojis, monospace font, markdown). Not trying to look like a food blog; trying to feel like *their* tool.

Emotional goals: comfort, familiarity, quiet delight. A small smile when you open it, not awe.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Base**: Clean, minimal, tool-first. The monochromatic foundation and heavy Geist Mono usage are correct and should be preserved.
- **Accent color**: Introduce one warm plant-based accent — a muted earthy green (sage/basil range, e.g. `oklch(0.52 0.1 145)`) to replace the flat neutral primary. Subtle, not loud.
- **Anti-references**: No food-blog maximalism (hero images, overlapping cards, cursive fonts, photography-driven layouts). No startup landing page energy.
- **Theme**: Both light and dark modes matter equally — they use both.
- **Visuals**: Emoji as first-class citizens. No stock photos. Markdown rendered cleanly with good typographic rhythm.

### Design Principles
1. **Tool over showcase** — Every design decision should serve the two people using it, not hypothetical visitors. Optimize for speed of use and comfort, not first impressions.
2. **Mono DNA** — Geist Mono is the personality. Use it consistently for headings, labels, metadata, and interactive elements. Body text and prose (recipe content) use Geist Sans.
3. **One warm accent, used sparingly** — The earthy green should feel like a seasoning, not a coat of paint. Reserve it for primary actions, active states, and key icons.
4. **Personal charm over production polish** — Emojis, Spanish copy, and the hand-curated feel are features, not quirks. Don't smooth them away.
5. **Accessible by conviction** — WCAG AA contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, and reduced motion respect are non-negotiable — not for compliance, but because that's the kind of developers they are.
